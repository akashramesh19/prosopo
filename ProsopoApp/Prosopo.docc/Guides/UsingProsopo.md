# Using Prosopo

Learn how to create, manage, and use personas in **Prosopo** for a consistent AI experience.

## Getting Started
1. Launch the **Prosopo** app.
2. Sign in with Apple or Google.
3. Once signed in, the **Home** page will display your personas.

## Create a Persona
1. Tap the **+ button** to add a new persona.
2. Enter the required details:
   - **Name**
   - **Role**
   - **Tone**
   - **Guardrails**
   - **Consistency slider**
3. Tap **Create** to generate a prompt and save the persona.

## View and Edit Personas
- Tap a persona tile to view its details.
- Use the **Edit** button in the top-right to make changes.
- Use **Copy & Open ChatGPT** or **Copy & Open Gemini** to test your persona prompts directly.

## Manage Personas
- Swipe left on a persona to **delete** it.
- Open the **menu drawer** (top-right) to log out or access the About page.

## Quick Gestures
- Long Press on a Persona tile to quick copy the prompt to clipboard.
- Double Tap on a Persona tile to go to edit page directly.
- Triple Tap on a Persona tile to delete the Persona.
